Questions before you book

Do I pay before I see the car?

No. You inspect it in daylight at pick up and pay only then.

Will you scratch the paint?

We never use brushes or automatic machines. Every panel is washed by hand with fresh mitts and two buckets with grit guards.

How long does a ceramic coating last?

Up to 3 years with a hand wash every two to four weeks. We give you a short care sheet at pick up.

Can you collect the car?

Yes, within 15 km of the studio. Mention it in the booking notes.

What if I need to move my slot?

Reschedule for free up to 24 hours before. Just reply to the confirmation message.

Do you work on leased cars?

Yes. A ceramic coating also helps at lease return, since the paint is assessed on condition.
